Frequently asked questions

Is this a scam?

A fair question, and we would rather answer it before you decide anything.

In this kind of work, brands pay people for the videos they use. Pured Media is a coaching program, not a brand, and it does not pay you. Whether a brand hires someone is the brand's decision.

The class is free to watch, and we do not ask for a card. At the end, Max also explains the coaching program, which has a fee. The price is on this page, and there is no obligation to join.

Is there an age limit?

Age is not a requirement for the class or for the coaching program. A big following is not required to learn this either.

What if I do not like being on camera?

Many people feel that way at first. Some videos show only your hands and the product. Others show your face, and each brand decides what it wants. In the coaching program, which has a fee, a coach reviews your work and helps you work within what you are comfortable with. You decide which videos you make and which deals you say yes to.
